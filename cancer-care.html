Cancer & Curative Care

Comprehensive cancer treatment support — from early diagnosis through chemotherapy, surgery, and recovery — for patients who cannot afford the journey to a cure.

Program Overview

Cancer is one of India's leading causes of mortality, yet the cost of treatment puts curative care out of reach for millions of families. Our Cancer & Curative Care program funds the full arc of cancer care — from diagnosis to treatment to recovery — so that a patient's financial situation does not determine whether they survive.

We partner with accredited oncology centres to fund treatment gaps, cover out-of-pocket costs, and support continuity of care for patients undergoing long-term treatment who would otherwise abandon it mid-course.

What We Fund

  • Biopsies, staging scans, and confirmatory diagnostics
  • Chemotherapy drugs and administration costs
  • Radiation therapy and targeted therapy sessions
  • Oncological surgeries and post-operative care
  • Palliative care and pain management support
  • Patient transport for treatment sessions at specialist centres

How Your Gift Helps

Cancer treatment is a long journey — your support helps patients finish it.

$75 — Diagnostic Tests

Covers biopsy or imaging scans needed to confirm a cancer diagnosis and determine the treatment path forward.

$300 — Chemotherapy Cycle

Funds one full chemotherapy cycle — medication, administration, and supportive care — for a patient undergoing curative treatment.

$2,000 — Full Treatment Course

Supports a complete curative treatment course for one cancer patient, from diagnosis through treatment completion.

Many patients abandon cancer treatment midway because they cannot afford to continue. Your donation ensures they make it to the end.
