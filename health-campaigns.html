Health Camps

Large-scale community health awareness, prevention screenings, and early-detection drives that reach people before illness becomes a crisis.

Program Overview

India's healthcare burden is shaped largely by conditions that are preventable or manageable when caught early. Our Health Camps program addresses this upstream — reaching communities with screenings, education, and outreach before conditions escalate into expensive, life-threatening emergencies.

We coordinate large-scale health camps, awareness drives, and referral pathways across rural and peri-urban India, partnering with local health workers, hospitals, and community organisations to maximise reach and sustained behaviour change.

What We Do

  • Community health screenings for diabetes, hypertension, TB, and anaemia
  • Cancer early-detection drives with referral support for confirmed cases
  • Maternal and child health awareness in rural and peri-urban areas
  • Hygiene, nutrition, and sanitation education campaigns
  • Mental health awareness and destigmatisation outreach
  • Vaccination and immunisation drives in underserved communities

Why Prevention Matters

Catching conditions early transforms outcomes — for patients, families, and the healthcare system.

Earlier Detection

Regular screening identifies conditions like hypertension, diabetes, and early-stage cancer at a point where treatment is simpler, cheaper, and more effective.

Community Reach

Campaigns bring healthcare directly to villages, schools, and workplaces — removing geography and cost as barriers to accessing basic health information.

Lower Long-term Cost

Prevention is many times cheaper than treatment. Every person whose condition is caught early reduces the financial burden on their family and on the healthcare system.

How Your Gift Helps

Every contribution funds real outreach that reaches people who would otherwise go unscreened.

$15 — One Screening

Covers a full health screening for one person — blood pressure, blood glucose, BMI check, and consultation with a trained health worker.

$100 — Community Camp

Funds a single-day community health camp serving up to 60 people with screenings, health education, and referrals to treatment where needed.

$500 — District Drive

Powers a multi-day awareness and screening drive across one district, reaching 300+ people with health education and early-detection services.
