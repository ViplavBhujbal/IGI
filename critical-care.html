Critical Care + Neonatal & Pediatric Care

Funding life-saving interventions for India's most critically ill patients — from premature newborns to children facing emergencies their families cannot afford.

Program Overview

Our Critical Care program provides life-saving interventions for patients requiring intensive medical support. We focus on neonatal and pediatric care — ensuring the youngest and most vulnerable receive timely, expert treatment regardless of their family's financial situation.

India has one of the world's highest rates of neonatal mortality, much of it preventable with adequate medical care. Our program bridges the funding gap so hospitals can provide the care patients need without cost being a barrier.

Key Focus Areas

  • Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) support for premature and critically ill newborns
  • Pediatric ICU care for children with life-threatening conditions
  • Cardiac care including surgeries for congenital heart defects
  • Respiratory support, ventilator care, and multi-organ management
  • Emergency surgical interventions for acute conditions
  • Transport support to reach specialist facilities in time

How Your Gift Helps

Every donation directly funds a specific aspect of critical care delivery.

$50 — One Day of NICU Support

Covers one day of incubator care, nutritional support, and monitoring for a premature newborn in a partner NICU facility.

$250 — Emergency Surgery

Helps fund an emergency surgical intervention for a child with a life-threatening condition who cannot otherwise access care.

$1,000 — Full ICU Course

Supports a complete pediatric ICU treatment course from admission through stabilization and discharge for one patient.

Every Child Deserves a Fighting Chance

Your donation funds critical care for children and newborns whose families cannot afford the treatment they urgently need.
