Our Programs

Four focused program areas where we create maximum impact on healthcare access and humanitarian relief.

Critical Care + Neonatal & Pediatric Care

Our Critical Care program provides life-saving interventions for patients requiring intensive medical care. We focus on neonatal and pediatric care, ensuring that the most vulnerable members of society receive timely, quality treatment.

Many families in India cannot afford the high costs of ICU care, leading to delayed or abandoned treatment. Our program bridges this gap by funding treatments at partner hospitals across the country.

Key Focus Areas

  • Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) support for premature and critically ill newborns
  • Pediatric ICU care for children with life-threatening conditions
  • Cardiac care including surgeries for congenital heart defects
  • Respiratory support and ventilator care
  • Multi-organ failure management and transplant support

Cancer & Curative Care

Cancer treatment in India remains inaccessible for millions due to high costs. Our Cancer & Curative Care program funds comprehensive cancer treatment for patients from economically disadvantaged backgrounds.

We partner with leading oncology centers to provide chemotherapy, radiation therapy, surgeries, and supportive care to patients who would otherwise have no access to treatment.

Key Focus Areas

  • Chemotherapy and targeted therapy treatments
  • Radiation therapy including advanced techniques
  • Surgical interventions for cancer removal
  • Bone marrow transplants for blood cancers
  • Palliative care and pain management
  • Post-treatment rehabilitation support

Disaster Relief & Emergency Response

When disasters strike, rapid response saves lives. Our Disaster Relief program provides immediate medical aid, essential supplies, and rehabilitation support to communities affected by natural disasters and emergencies.

We work with local partners and government agencies to ensure aid reaches those who need it most, quickly and efficiently.

Key Focus Areas

  • Emergency medical camps in disaster-affected areas
  • Distribution of essential medicines and medical supplies
  • Clean water and sanitation support
  • Temporary shelter and rehabilitation assistance
  • Mental health and psychosocial support
  • Long-term community recovery programs

Health Camps

Our Health Camps program drives large-scale awareness, prevention, and early-intervention initiatives across communities in India. We bring healthcare information and basic services directly to people before illness becomes crisis.

Many conditions that require expensive critical or curative care are preventable with timely screening, education, and community outreach. Our campaigns reduce the downstream burden on families, hospitals, and the healthcare system as a whole.

Key Focus Areas

  • Community health screenings for diabetes, hypertension, TB, and anaemia
  • Maternal and child health awareness programs in rural and peri-urban areas
  • Cancer early-detection drives and referral support for confirmed cases
  • Hygiene, nutrition, and sanitation education campaigns
  • Mental health awareness and destigmatisation outreach
  • Vaccination and immunisation drives in underserved communities
