About Impact Guru Inc.

Impact Guru Inc. is a registered US 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to expanding healthcare access for vulnerable communities in India.

Our Mission

Impact Guru Inc. exists to ensure that no family in India loses a loved one because they cannot afford life-saving medical care. We bridge the gap between donors who want to create real change and patients who need urgent healthcare support.

Founded in 2015, we have grown from a small volunteer network into a structured 501(c)(3) nonprofit with a track record of transparent, high-impact healthcare philanthropy — connecting US donors and corporate partners with verified programs across India.

  • Founded 2015 — over nine years of verified healthcare impact
  • Registered US 501(c)(3) — EIN: 87-3861987
  • 87% of every dollar goes directly to programs
  • GuideStar Platinum transparency rating
  • Verified on Benevity, YourCause, CyberGrants, and Bright Funds

Our Core Values

Every decision we make is guided by these principles.

Transparency First

We publish annual audited financials, governance documents, and program-level fund allocation reports. Donors deserve to know exactly where their money goes.

Healthcare as a Right

We believe access to quality medical care is a fundamental human right — not a privilege reserved for those who can afford it. Cost should never be a death sentence.

Community Driven

Our programs are shaped by the communities we serve. We partner with local hospitals, healthcare workers, and community organizations to ensure funds create lasting change.
