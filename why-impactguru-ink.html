Why Impact Guru Inc.

A trusted partner in healthcare philanthropy, combining expertise with transparency to create lasting impact.

Our Mission

To ensure no family is denied healthcare because of financial barriers.

Impact Guru Inc. was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to bridge the gap between those who need healthcare and those who can help provide it. We believe that access to medical care is a fundamental right, not a privilege.

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in the United States, we channel donor generosity to support healthcare access and humanitarian relief programs in India, where millions of families face impossible choices between their health and their livelihoods.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every decision we make and every program we run.

Healthcare Expertise

Deep understanding of healthcare systems in India with established partnerships across 165+ hospitals and medical institutions.

Measurable Impact

Every program is tracked with verified metrics. We report outcomes, not just activities, so donors see exactly how their support creates change.

Full Transparency

Complete financial disclosure, independently audited statements, and regular reporting on how every dollar is utilized.

Trusted Implementation

Rigorous vetting of partner hospitals, ongoing monitoring, and strong governance ensure that programs deliver real results.

What Sets Us Apart

Our unique approach combines the best practices of healthcare philanthropy with the accountability donors expect.

Direct Hospital Partnerships

Unlike platforms that rely solely on individual fundraising, we work directly with hospitals to identify patients who need help but have no means to pay. This ensures funds go to verified medical cases.

  • 165+ partner hospitals across India
  • Direct disbursement to hospitals, not individuals
  • Verified medical documentation for every case
  • Ongoing relationship management and quality assurance

Comprehensive Impact Tracking

We don't just report how much money was raised—we track and report actual patient outcomes, treatment success rates, and long-term impact on beneficiary families.

  • Real-time beneficiary tracking system
  • Treatment outcome monitoring
  • Follow-up assessments for long-term impact
  • Quarterly impact reports for all donors

Healthcare Expertise

Our team includes healthcare professionals who understand the complexities of medical treatment in India. This expertise helps us identify high-impact interventions and ensure appropriate care.

  • Medical advisory board with specialists
  • Treatment protocol review and guidance
  • Cost validation for all funded treatments
  • Quality standards for partner hospitals

Emergency Response Capability

When disasters strike, we can mobilize rapidly. Our established networks and pre-positioned resources enable fast, effective emergency response.

  • 24-48 hour response time for emergencies
  • Pre-established local partner networks
  • Medical supply and equipment inventory
  • Trained emergency response teams
